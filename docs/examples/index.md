**Fragility guide.** One example per data type on simulated data: multiple-stripe counts,
field surveys with clustered events, IDA capacities with censoring, cloud analysis, ordered
damage states, bootstrap and Bayesian fits, and reporting.

**Paper example.** The wood-frame case study of Dahal, Burton & Onyambu (2022): eight
buildings, MLE versus sandwich uncertainty, and their effect on the mean annual frequency of
collapse.
